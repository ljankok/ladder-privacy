Ladder Calisthenics – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Ladder Calisthenics is a bodyweight strength training app. It is designed so that your data never leaves your phone.

What the app stores

Everything you enter or log is stored only on your device: your set-up answers (sex, age, height, weight, health check, medication classes, training background), your training plan, exercise logs, daily entries (weight, steps, walks, sleep, notes), and your settings. The app has no account, no sign-in, and no server of its own.

What the app does not do

  • It does not send your data anywhere. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • It does not use iCloud. Your data is not synced or backed up by the app. (Your normal iPhone backup may include it, under your own Apple ID and your own control.)
  • It does not sell, share, or monetise anything.

Apple Health

If you turn on Health sync in Settings, the app reads your daily step count and body weight from Apple Health and, when you choose, writes your morning weight and finished strength sessions back to Apple Health. Health data is used only inside the app and is never transmitted. You can change or revoke this permission at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Calendar and notifications

If you export your plan to the Calendar app, the app creates and updates its own events there; it does not read other events. Reminders are local notifications generated on your phone.

Exports

The CSV and PDF exports are files you create yourself. They go only where you choose to send them.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset removes everything the app has stored. Deleting the app removes all of it, including any backups the app kept of its own data file.

Medical note

Ladder Calisthenics is not a medical device and gives no medical advice. Its safety notes are general precautions; talk to your doctor before starting if the health check advises it.
